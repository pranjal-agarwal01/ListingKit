This Privacy Policy explains what information ListingKit ("we", "us") handles when you use our tool, and how. We've written it to reflect how the Service actually operates today.

1. The short version

  • We currently do not require an account and do not ask for personal login details.
  • The listing details you type are sent to a third-party AI provider (Google) to generate your content, then returned to you.
  • We do not store your listing inputs or generated kits on our own servers — generation happens in-request and the result is delivered to your browser.
  • Please don't enter sensitive personal information (see the AI note below).

2. Information we handle

  • Listing and brand details you enter: such as property address, price, features, and the agent name, handle, phone number, and brand color you provide. These are used only to generate your marketing kit.
  • Basic technical data: like standard server logs (e.g., IP address, browser type, timestamps) generated by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
  • Payment information (only if/when paid plans launch): handled entirely by a third-party merchant of record. We do not collect or store your card details.

3. How your inputs are processed by AI

To generate content, the details you enter are transmitted to Google's Gemini API. Your inputs are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. Depending on the API tier in use, Google may process, retain, or review submitted data to operate and improve its services. Because of this, do not enter confidential or sensitive personal information (for example, a client's private financial or personal data) into the tool. Only enter listing details you're comfortable sharing publicly, since they're destined for public marketing anyway.

4. Service providers we use

  • Google (Gemini API) — generates the marketing content from your inputs.
  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) — serves the website and runs the generation function.
  • A merchant of record (when payments launch) — processes subscriptions and taxes.

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies.

5. How we use information

Only to (a) provide the Service and generate your requested content, (b) keep the Service secure and reliable, and (c) comply with law. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data retention

We do not maintain a database of your listing inputs or generated kits on our servers today. Transient processing data and hosting logs are retained only as long as needed for operation and security. Third-party providers retain data under their own policies.

7. Cookies and analytics

The current Service uses minimal or no cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this Policy and, where required, present a consent choice.

8. Your choices and rights

Since we don't store your inputs, the simplest control is to only enter what you're comfortable with. Depending on where you live (for example, under CCPA in California or GDPR in the EU), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Children

The Service is intended for real estate professionals and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. International processing

The Service and its providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using the Service, you understand your information may be processed outside your country of residence.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
